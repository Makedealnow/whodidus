Make Thune call the vote

The full-year funding bills can pass before the December 11 deadline — if the Senate sets the filibuster aside and votes. Your two senators are the pressure. Pick your state, then email or call them. Two minutes.

Senators mostly answer their own constituents — that's why yours are the two who count. Names, phone numbers, and contact links come from the U.S. Senate's official directory at senate.gov.
